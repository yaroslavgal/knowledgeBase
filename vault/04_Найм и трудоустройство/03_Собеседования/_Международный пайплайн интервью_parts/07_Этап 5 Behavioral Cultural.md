## Этап 5: Behavioral / Cultural

### STAR метод

- **S**ituation — контекст
- **T**ask — задача
- **A**ction — что ты сделал (главное!)
- **R**esult — что получилось (с цифрами)

### Топ-15 вопросов

1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager
2. Describe a project you led from start to finish
3. How do you handle conflicts in the team?
4. Tell me about a time you failed
5. Tell me about a time you delivered under tight deadline
6. Describe a situation when you had to learn new technology fast
7. How do you give feedback to underperforming team member?
8. Tell me about a time you had to convince others
9. Describe a complex technical decision you made
10. Tell me about a time you mentored someone
11. How do you handle ambiguity / unclear requirements?
12. Describe a time you simplified a complex problem
13. Tell me about a time you had to push back
14. How do you balance speed vs quality?
15. Tell me about your proudest achievement

**Подготовь 10–12 STAR-историй** — они закроют 80% вопросов через переиспользование.

### Amazon Leadership Principles

Если идёшь в Amazon — учи 14 LP. На каждый вопрос ожидают конкретный LP-ответ.
- Customer Obsession
- Ownership
- Invent and Simplify
- Are Right, A Lot
- Learn and Be Curious
- Hire and Develop the Best
- Insist on the Highest Standards
- Think Big
- Bias for Action
- Frugality
- Earn Trust
- Dive Deep
- Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
- Deliver Results
